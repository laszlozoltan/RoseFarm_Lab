Introduction

Here at the RoseFarm Lab our research primarily centers on elucidating the biochemical responses of wild roses to herbivory. Through carefully designed experimental setups, we analyse the changes in secondary metabolites and other compounds triggered by herbivore interactions. This approach enables us to gain insights into the defence mechanisms employed by wild roses and their potential implications for various industries.
