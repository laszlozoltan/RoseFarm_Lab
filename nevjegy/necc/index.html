The NECC (Nature Education Community Center) was founded in November 2017 as an initiative of the students of the Faculty of Biology and Geology of the Babeș-Bolyai University of Cluj-Napoca and the professor Kunigunda Macalik, who runs the interactive play house "Wonders of a Thousand Faces of Nature". The aim of the initiative is to raise awareness and popularize science through nature education activities, where learning through play plays an important role.

Details: https://www.neccesek.com/

Nestled in the serene countryside of Lopadea, Romania, Molnar Garden is dedicated to roses. With a deep-rooted passion for horticulture and appreciation for the timeless elegance of roses, we specialize in nurturing these exquisite blooms to perfection. Whether you're drawn to the allure of classic reds, the romance of soft pinks, the vibrancy of sunny yellows, or the delicate charm of pristine whites, we're certain to have the perfect roses that align with your personal taste and aesthetic preferences. From handpicking the ideal rose varieties suited to your local climate and soil conditions to offering invaluable insights on planting, pruning, and ongoing care, we're committed to assisting you in crafting a breath taking rose garden that will leave your neighbour's in awe.

Details: https://molnargarden.ro/
