Metabolites and extracts

Wild rose metabolites and extractions, particularly as oils, a treasure of natural compounds have diverse applications in various industries. These metabolites encompass a wide range of bioactive substances, including essential oils, fatty acids, phenolic compounds, and antioxidants, among others. Extracted from various parts of the wild rose plant, such as petals, hips, and seeds, these metabolites possess potent medicinal, cosmetic, and culinary properties.

Wild rose essential oil, extracted from rose petals through steam distillation, is renowned for its enchanting fragrance and therapeutic benefits. Rich in compounds like geraniol, citronellol, and nerol, rose essential oil exhibits antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties, making it a valuable ingredient in aromatherapy, skincare, and natural perfumery.

Rosehip oil, derived from the seeds of wild rose hips, is prized for its high content of essential fatty acids, vitamins, and antioxidants. It is widely used in skincare formulations, including moisturizers, serums, and anti-aging treatments.

Beyond skincare, wild rose metabolites find applications in pharmaceuticals, nutraceuticals, and culinary products. Rosehip extracts, rich in vitamin C, flavonoids, and carotenoids, are utilized in dietary supplements and herbal remedies for their immune-boosting and antioxidant properties. Additionally, rosehip-infused products, such as teas, jams, and syrups, offer a flavorful and nutritious addition to the culinary world.
